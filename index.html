Privacy Policy for Monied

Last updated: October 2026

1. Data collection (none)

We do not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data, financial data, or device information. This matters especially here: your transactions and budget never leave your device.

2. Local storage only

Everything you enter — transactions, budget, settings — is stored exclusively on your own device, in a local SQLite database (via the Drift database library), inside the app's private, sandboxed storage. We have no servers, no databases, and no access to your information. We never connect to your bank, card, or any financial institution — every entry is typed in by you.

3. No cookies, no tracking

We do not use cookies, web beacons, fingerprinting, or any tracking technologies. The app makes no network requests except the one described below. The only persistent data on your device is your transactions and settings.

4. Third-party services

We do not integrate with any analytics, crash-reporting, or advertising SDKs. The only external service is Google Play, which processes your one-time Monied Pro purchase through the Google Play Billing Library. Buying Pro, restoring it and checking that you have bought it go through it. We do not receive or store your payment details: those are handled entirely by Google, and Google's own privacy policy applies to that part. The app has no internet permission of its own.

5. Things you start that leave the app

The app never sends anything by itself. These hand over to another app only when you choose: exporting a CSV or sharing a monthly summary (to the sharing app you pick), and rating the app (four or five stars open the Google Play page so you can leave a public review; a lower rating with a comment opens an email draft to the developer, and the comment is only sent if you send that email, so please do not put account numbers or other private details in it).

6. Home screen widget, notifications and recent apps

The optional widget shows this month's spending against your budget. It is worked out on your phone from numbers the app saves in its private storage, and nothing is sent anywhere. Anyone who can see your home screen can see it, so "Hide amounts on the widget" makes it show only a percentage. "Hide Monied in recent apps" keeps the app out of the recent-apps list and screenshots. Android's own backup is switched off for this app, so your data is not copied to the cloud or to a new phone.

7. Children's privacy

Monied is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from anyone of any age — consistent with Section 1, we collect no data at all.

8. Your rights (GDPR / CCPA / POPIA)

Because we hold zero data about you, there is nothing to access, rectify, or delete. All your data is under your physical control on your device. To delete it, uninstall the app or use "Clear all data" inside Settings.

9. Changes to this policy

If we ever update this policy — for example, adding an optional cloud feature — we will flag it clearly in the app. This version remains valid for the fully offline version of the app.
